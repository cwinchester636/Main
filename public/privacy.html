SwapDeck Privacy Policy

Last updated September 22, 2026

SwapDeck ("the app," "we," "us") is a trading card collection and trading app. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and how it's handled. SwapDeck is a small, independently operated app — this policy describes exactly what the app actually does, not boilerplate.

Information you provide directly

  • Account info: username, email address, and password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text).
  • Profile: an avatar you choose from a built-in set, and an optional ZIP code.
  • Collection data: the cards you mark as owned ("Haves") or wanted ("Wants"), including condition and grade if you enter them.
  • Verification photos: photos you optionally attach to a card to prove you own it. These are visible to other users who are matched to trade for that card, and to admins reviewing a dispute.
  • Trade activity: trade proposals, chat messages between trading partners, ratings and written reviews you leave after a trade, and any fraud/dispute reports you file.
  • Local events: if you post a local card event, its title, date, location, and description are visible to other users.

Information collected automatically

  • Approximate location: your ZIP code (if you provide one) is converted to a rough latitude/longitude centroid, used only to show approximate distance to other collectors and events. SwapDeck never requests or collects your device's precise GPS location.
  • Push notification subscription: if you enable notifications, the browser/device token needed to deliver them.
  • Purchase data: if you buy SwapDeck Pro, RevenueCat and Google Play process the transaction; SwapDeck's servers only receive confirmation that a purchase entitlement is active, never your payment card details.
  • IP address at signup: stored only to detect referral-reward abuse (e.g. the same person creating multiple accounts to repeatedly claim a "refer a friend" bonus). Not used for anything else, and not the same as tracking your ongoing location or activity.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data to anyone.
  • We don't use advertising trackers or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • We don't request precise device location.

Who can see what

Your username, avatar, approximate distance, collection lists, and rating history are visible to other SwapDeck users as part of the app's core matching/trading function. Chat messages are visible only to you and the trading partner in that conversation, plus an admin if a fraud report is filed on that specific trade. Your email address is never shown to other users.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept as long as your account exists. You can delete your account yourself at any time from Profile → Delete my account, or by contacting us (see below); either way, this permanently removes your account, collection, messages, and photos from our systems.

Third-party services

SwapDeck runs on Cloudflare (hosting, database, and file storage) and uses free public card-database APIs (the Pokémon TCG API, Scryfall, and YGOPRODeck) to look up card data — these lookups don't send any of your personal information, only the card name you search for. In-app purchases are processed by Google Play and RevenueCat.

Age requirement

SwapDeck is intended for users 18 and older. Creating an account requires confirming you meet this age requirement, and the app is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change and the update will be posted here.

Contact
